# Onboarding intake form

The structured checklist behind Step 1. Use this to either parse a pasted/uploaded
completed form, or to ask the same questions live, batched by section. Never ask
all fields one at a time — batch by section, confirm, move on.

If the user has a completed doc (txt, Google Doc, PDF), ask them to paste or
upload it, then extract every field below from it directly. Only ask live for
whatever the doc leaves blank. Flag required fields (marked *) that are still
missing after parsing, and ask for just those.

## Section A: Contact & company details

- Full name *
- Email address *
- Phone number *
- Company name *
- Company address *
- Company website *
- Company logo (Drive link, optional)
- Project leader email *
- Full name for the sending inboxes *
- Job title for the inbox *

Low-signal for the ICP itself, but required to produce the doc and, later, the
campaign assets. Ask as one batch, move on fast.

## Section B: Company overview

- Offer and business details, in detail *
- Benefits of the offer *
- Any offer/result guarantee? *
- What sets them apart from competitors or alternative methods? *
- Working lead magnets, or ideas for lead magnets? *
- Additional marketing assets (brand guide, decks, one-pagers) (optional)

This is the offer half of Step 1's old baseline ask. If they have no assets yet,
note that and continue.

## Section C: ICP information

- Ideal client criteria, as specific as possible *
- Who is NOT the ideal client? *
- ICP company examples (URLs) — this absorbs the old "top 10%, highest LTV,
  fastest close, lowest churn" ask. If they have a customer list instead of
  named examples, that's the CSV import path (Step 0), not this field.
- Location of clients *
- Ideal company size *
- Job titles to target *
- Department / function *
- Seniority to reach out to *
- Industries to target *
- Industries to exclude *
- Keywords to look for on a prospect's website/LinkedIn to gauge fit *
- Prospects' main worries, pain points, and desires — spare no detail *
- Why working with them is BETTER than the current alternative or competitor,
  and what the current way is costing the prospect *
- The perfect buying state of the prospect, described in full *
- Signals that a prospect can use the solution (headcount growth, funding, a
  policy change, etc.) *
- Additional ICP notes *
- Questions prospects respond well to *
- Case studies with this ICP, or links to them *

This is the core of the ICP baseline. If they have no customer data (early
stage), the "ICP company examples" and "case studies" fields can come back
blank — fall back to company website + offer and build from market research
instead, same as before.

## After intake

Once Sections A-C are captured (parsed or asked), do not re-ask any of this in
Steps 2-8. Step 2 pattern-matches against Section C's criteria and examples,
Step 3 researches the pains named in Section C in the prospect's own words, and
Section B's differentiators and guarantees feed the competitor-gap and offer
work directly.
