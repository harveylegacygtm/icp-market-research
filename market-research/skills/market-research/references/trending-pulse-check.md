# Trending topics pulse check

Not the same job as "Industry triggers" in references/sop-full.md. Industry
triggers are macro trends used to score lead urgency at the company level
(AI adoption, cost pressure, new regulation) with a 12-24 month lens. This pulse
check is narrower and faster-moving: what is this specific ICP/persona actually
talking about right now, this month, that a cold email could reference to prove
you're paying attention. It feeds hooks, opening lines, and Big Ideas — not
lead scoring.

Example: for cold email agencies and B2B outbound operators in 2026, that's
Google's deliverability and bulk-sender requirements — not "email marketing
trends" in general.

## Window

Last 30-90 days. Tighter than the skill's standing 12-24 month research window
everywhere else. If nothing genuinely new happened in that window, say so and
widen to 6 months rather than stretch a stale story to look current.

## What to search (live web search, not training knowledge)

- Platform or regulatory changes that hit this ICP's tools or workflow (a
  sending-platform policy update, a compliance deadline, a new law).
- Industry news: funding waves, layoffs, M&A, notable product launches in
  their category.
- What's actually being discussed where they hang out: recent LinkedIn posts
  from people with this job title, recent Reddit/niche-forum threads, recent
  podcast episodes — look for the same topic surfacing more than once.
- Conferences or industry events in the last quarter that generated buzz.

Query patterns: "[industry] [job title] news [current month] [year]",
"[industry] regulation OR policy change [year]", site:reddit.com "[industry]"
recent, site:linkedin.com "[job title]" [topic].

## Output format

3-5 trending topics, each with:

- **What it is**: one sentence.
- **Source + date**: URL and publish date, inside the 30-90 day window.
- **Why this ICP cares**: the specific mechanism connecting the topic to their
  day-to-day, not a generic "this affects the industry."
- **How to use it**: pick one — hook/opener line, urgency framing for the
  Signal-to-Offer map, a Big Idea "borrowed interest" angle, or a risk-factor
  flag if the topic would make this ICP defensive or skeptical instead of
  receptive.

Cite every source. If a topic can't be traced to a dated source in the window,
leave it out rather than including something that reads current but isn't.
